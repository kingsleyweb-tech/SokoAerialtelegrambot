## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

**Name:** Soko Aerial (also styled "SoKo Aerial")
**Location:** Burma Camp,Accra, Ghana
**Tagline:** "Aerial Imaging In Africa"
**Founded:** 2017
**Founder:** Ing. Kofi Owusu-Adusei
**CEO:** Ing. Kofi Owusu-Adusei
**What they do:** SoKo Aerial is an end-to-end commercial enterprise specializing in precision aerial inspections and survey data. They use highly sophisticated Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs/drones) to collect aerial data, then process, analyze, and distribute that data to suit clients' needs across various industries.

**Vision:** To become the premier service provider using UAV systems to support industry and security within the West Africa (WA) Region and achieve optimum goals.

**Mission:** To aggressively penetrate and introduce UAV systems to support industry and security in West Africa by providing specialized aerial solutions that directly impact businesses and communities.

**Strategic Partners:**
- MATE Engineering Limited
- RECS Geomatics Consult
- Signal Training School – Ghana Armed Forces

The company states it has developed proven solutions in construction, survey, railway, and infrastructure sectors in partnership with industry experts, and has carried out pilot projects to explore UAV solutions that improve safety and efficiency.

## 2. INDUSTRIES / SERVICES OFFERED

### Mining
Aerial data used to mitigate risk and maximize ROI by replacing hazardous manual inspections. Capabilities:
- Equipment & Operation Monitoring
- Geotechnical & Environmental Monitoring
- Security and Surveillance
- Incident Emergency Response
- Digital Terrain Modeling
- Construction Management
[Full PDF available](https://sokoaerial.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Mining.pdf)

### Disaster Data Management
A data collection and management system for agencies managing disasters, covering 3 activity stages:
- Pre-disaster activities
- Activities during disaster management
- Post-disaster activities
Services provided at each stage: photogrammetry, orthophotos for infrastructure, drone data analysis, and aerial imagery — used to help plan/redesign flood-prone areas and safeguard life/property during the rainy season.
[PDF available](https://sokoaerial.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Disaster-Data-Management.pdf)

### Infrastructure Inspection
Drone-based inspection for industrial plants, factories, workshop buildings, elevated tanks, bridges, wind turbines, pylons, technical building infrastructure, leisure/tourism and sports facilities. Benefits:
- Improves safety (no need to put people in unsafe situations)
- Saves time and reduces costs
- Live video feed viewing
- Operator can be directed to specific areas of interest
- Video playback for defect/abnormality analysis
- Transportable and flying within minutes
[Full PDF available](https://sokoaerial.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/infrastructure-inspection-using-UAV.pdf)

### Agriculture
Drones used to increase crop production and monitor crop growth via advanced sensors/digital imaging. Capabilities:
- Retrieve Aerial View of Fields
- Multispectral Images
- Apply Insecticides
[Full PDF available](https://sokoaerial.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Agriculture.pdf)

### Security
Drones provide aerial surveillance beyond traditional ground-based methods, offering wide field of view, discreet/efficient coverage, and sensors that detect what the human eye cannot. Capabilities:
- Reconnaissance
- Situational Awareness
- Target Acquisition
- Crowd Monitoring
[Full PDF available](https://sokoaerial.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Aerial-Surveillance-and-Security.pdf)

### Survey & Mapping
Fast, accurate, and cost-effective mapping using UAV survey technology, replacing expensive/time-consuming/dangerous traditional methods. Benefits:
- Fast & Accurate Data Acquisition
- Cost-effectiveness
- Digital web platform
- Boost in productivity
[Full PDF available](https://sokoaerial.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Suvey-Mapping.pdf)

### Architecture, Engineering & Construction (AEC)
Drones assist with site inspection, planning, and health & safety by providing a bird's-eye view of construction sites. Used to quickly survey job sites and build maps, reducing need for human resources, heavy machinery, and expensive surveying tools — completing projects in less time, at lower cost, with more accurate results.
[Full PDF available](https://sokoaerial.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Drone-Technology-in-Architecture.pdf)

## 3. INTERNAL DEPARTMENTS

### Commercial Aerial Data Unit (CADU)
**Mission:** Provide all needed drone data solutions ranging from mapping, inspection, and others.
**Drone Fleet & Data Processing System:**
- Arace fixed wing
- DJI Mavic Pro drones
- Arace Evo Multicopter
- 2 desktop processing machines

### Unmanned Aerial System Research Lab (UASRL)
**Mission:** Serve as a hub for drone-related activities; provides drones, sensors, equipment, software, and knowledge to support high-quality research and teaching across civil, military, and university sectors.
**Drone Service and Maintenance Centre:** Drone repair service, software upgrades, configuration, and testing.
**Research Projects:**
- Designing/developing fixed-wing training models
- Designing/developing multi-copter training models
- Developing aero model lab tools and kits

**Featured Project — AHUODEN:** A high-altitude surveillance drone modeled after the American Global Hawk drone. Aims to address the need for high-endurance, high-altitude drones for a wide range of missions. Status: completed all ground tests successfully, awaiting maiden flight. It will be used to test flight control software and onboard sensors/instruments. A more advanced, higher-endurance version is currently in the design phase.

### Education and Training Unit (ENTU)
**Mission:** Develop state-of-the-art training materials and study guides for educational support of the UAV industry in Ghana and beyond.
**Current Programs:**
- Drone piloting and control system
- Drone data processing and GIS
**Upcoming Programs:**
- Certificate of Science in Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems Engineering

### Communication Engineering and Software Unit (CESU)
**Mission:** Develop ground control stations, drone tracking systems, and counter-drone technology support solutions for both private and military use.
**Research Projects:**
- Offline tracking system for flyaway drones
- HIL (Hardware-in-the-Loop) ground control station for pilot training and simulation
- QR Code ID card system
- Pizza ordering and delivery app

## 4. TRAINING PROGRAMS

**Centre for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles Research and Education (CUAVRE):** A learning center focused on teaching new pilots the technology, with emphasis on piloting, data acquisition, data analysis, GIS, and engineering/design.

**Drone Pilot Training:** Designed for corporations building pilot teams and individuals seeking careers in drone applications. Offered via online learning and on-site, hands-on flight training.

**Training Objectives:**
- **Safety** – Safe flying and emergency response
- **Solution** – Understanding commercial/industrial solutions and latest drone technology
- **Operation** – Learning piloting apps, data acquisition, and processing
- **Maintenance** – Standard equipment maintenance and operations
- **Mission** – Using drones to capture mission data for valuable insights

**On-site training:** Live demonstration, comprehensive flight operations, equipment maintenance, and emergency response practice — held at the center or at a client's specified venue.

**Online training:** Unlimited access to study materials plus on-demand virtual classes.
