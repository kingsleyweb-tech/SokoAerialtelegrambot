## 2. INDUSTRIES / SERVICES OFFERED

### Mining
Aerial data used to mitigate risk and maximize ROI by replacing hazardous manual inspections. Capabilities:
- Equipment & Operation Monitoring
- Geotechnical & Environmental Monitoring
- Security and Surveillance
- Incident Emergency Response
- Digital Terrain Modeling
- Construction Management
- Mitigating risk and maximizing return on investment by replacing potentially hazardous manual inspections for collecting critical information using drones will increase efficiency, improve safety, streamline operations and reduce costs.

### Disaster Data Management
SoKo Aerial presents a convenient, effective data collection and management system that can be employed by agencies in managing disasters. The system provides aerial data solutions across 3 activities in disaster management:
- Pre-disaster activities
- Activities during disaster management (managing disaster)
- Post-disaster activities
At each activity stage, SoKo Aerial provides photogrammetry, orthophotos for infrastructure, drone data analysis, and aerial imagery that is set to assist disaster management and environmental protection agencies in the implementation of practices needed for planning and redesigning flood-prone areas so as to safeguard life and properties during the rainy season.

### Infrastructure Inspection
Drones are designed for easy use, quick deployment, and intense accuracy. Drones are used for accurate and risk-free vertical inspection of industrial plants, factories, workshop buildings, elevated tanks, bridge construction, wind turbines, pylons, technical building infrastructure, leisure/tourism and sports facilities. Since these facilities are subject to strict safety and maintenance, they must be inspected regularly. 
Benefits of using a UAV in Vertical Infrastructure Inspection:
- Improves safety: no need to put people in unsafe situations.
- Saves time and reduces costs.
- Ability to view live video feeds.
- You can direct the UAV operator to specific areas of interest.
- View back videos to identify defects and abnormalities for instant analysis.
- Transportable and flying within minutes.

### Agriculture
Today drones are applied to farming in order to help increase crop production and monitor crop growth. Through the use of advanced sensors and digital imaging capabilities, farmers are able to use these drones to help them gather a richer picture of their fields to boost yields and farm efficiency. Capabilities:
- Retrieve Aerial View of Fields
- Multispectral Images
- Apply Insecticides

### Security
Unmanned aircraft systems (drones) provide the ideal solution to the problems and limitations faced by the traditional surveillance methods by providing an aerial perspective from which they can be able to make very insightful decisions and strategies. Once airborne, a drone provides a very wide field of view which the personnel on the ground would not have, and with such a wide coverage from a distance, surveillance can be conducted discreetly and efficiently. Small lightweight drones are also equipped with sophisticated cameras and sensors that are able to provide imagery that the human eye is unable to detect. Capabilities:
- Reconnaissance
- Situational Awareness
- Target Acquisition
- Crowd Monitoring

### Survey & Mapping
Surveying and mapping are not only expensive and time-consuming tasks but also very difficult and often dangerous. Traditionally, creating Digital Terrain Models and Base Maps has been expensive and time-consuming, but with our UAV mining surveys and aerial data technology, we can provide extremely fast, accurate, and cost-effective mapping results. Our aerial data solutions application for survey and mapping is designed to increase efficiency in data collection while minimizing the risks and costly expenses of traditional inspections and monitoring.
Benefits clients stand to gain include:
- Fast & Accurate Data Acquisition
- Cost-effectiveness
- Digital web platform
- Boost in productivity

### Architecture, Engineering & Construction (AEC)
Drones assist with site inspection, planning, and health & safety by providing a bird's-eye view of construction sites. Used to quickly survey job sites and build maps, reducing need for human resources, heavy machinery, and expensive surveying tools — completing projects in less time, at lower cost, with more accurate results.

## 3. INTERNAL DEPARTMENTS

### Commercial Aerial Data Unit (CADU)
**Mission:** Provide all needed drone data solutions ranging from mapping, inspection, and others.
**Drone Fleet & Data Processing System:**
- Arace fixed wing
- DJI Mavic Pro drones
- Arace Evo Multicopter
- 2 desktop processing machines

### Unmanned Aerial System Research Lab (UASRL)
**Mission:** Serve as a hub for drone-related activities and provide a pool of drones, sensors, equipment, software, and knowledge to support the use of drones in high-quality research and teaching across the civil space, military, and University departments.
**Drone Service and Maintenance Centre:** Drone repair service, software upgrades, configuration, and testing.
**Research Projects:**
- Designing and developing fixed-wing training models
- Designing and developing multi-copter training models
- Developing aero model lab tools and kits
- **Featured Project — AHUODEN:** A high-altitude surveillance drone modeled exactly after the American Global Hawk drone. The AHUODEN model seeks to provide insight into the ever-increasing need for high endurance and high altitude drones that can serve in a wide range of missions. Status: completed all ground tests successfully and awaiting its maiden flight. It will be used as a platform to test the capabilities of various flight control software as well as on-board sensors and instruments. A more advanced version with very high endurance capability is currently in the design phase.

### Education and Training Unit (ENTU)
**Mission:** Help develop state-of-the-art training materials and study guides for educational support for the UAV industry in Ghana and beyond.
**Current Programs:**
- Drone piloting and control system
- Drone data processing and GIS
**Upcoming Programs:**
- Certificate of Science in Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems Engineering.

### Communication Engineering and Software Unit (CESU)
**Mission:** Help develop ground control stations, drone tracking systems, and counter-drone technology support solutions for both private and military use.
**Research Projects:**
- Offline tracking system for flyaway drones
- Developing of HIL (Hardware-in-the-Loop) ground control station for Pilot training and simulation
- QR Code ID card system
- Pizza ordering and delivery app

## 4. TRAINING PROGRAMS & CUAVRE

### Centre for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles Research and Education (CUAVRE)
SoKo Aerial’s Centre for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles Research and Education (CUAVRE) is a learning center built to harness the potentials of Emerging Technologies. Drones are a key tool when it comes to industries of today, and the aim of this center is to teach and introduce to new pilots the ins and outs of the technology with emphasis on piloting, data acquisition, data analysis, GIS, and Engineering and Design.

### Essence of Training
Technology has enriched our lives with greater convenience, improved access to information, and revolutionized how we work. Developing skills and acquiring training puts you ahead of the competition and makes you relevant in today’s industries and the future of work.

### Drone Pilot Training
The course is developed for corporations who plan to build up a pilot team and individuals that can achieve the best possibilities for drone applications. Through our online learning and on-site, hands-on flight training, our trainees will acquire the comprehensive skills needed to become professional pilots.

### Training Objectives
- **SAFETY:** Safe flying and how to react during emergency situations.
- **SOLUTION:** Understanding commercial and industrial solutions as well as the latest Drone Technology.
- **OPERATION:** Learning about the piloting apps and how to acquire and process.
- **MAINTENANCE:** Standard equipment maintenance and operations.
- **MISSION:** Utilising Drones to capture mission data for valuable insights.

### On-Site Training
On-site training includes a live demonstration, comprehensive flight operations, equipment maintenance, and reacting to emergency situations. This can be done at the center or at a client's specified venue.

### Online Training
Unlimited access to our study materials as well as on-demand virtual classes.

## 8. FAQs

**Q: What is Soko Aerial?**
A: Soko Aerial is a commercial enterprise established in 2017 that specializes in precision aerial inspections and survey data using Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs/drones). It collects, processes, analyzes, and distributes aerial data to suit clients' needs across various industries.

**Q: What does the name "Soko" mean?**
A: "Soko" is a Swahili word that translates to "a marketplace" or "a place of trade." In Soko Aerial's context, it reflects the company's vision of creating a central hub connecting top talent, advanced drone technology, and professional aerial solutions with industry needs.

**Q: Where is Soko Aerial located?**
A: Supply Bus Stop, Burma Camp, Accra, Ghana.

**Q: How can I contact Soko Aerial?**

**Q: What industries does Soko Aerial serve?**
A: Mining, Disaster Data Management, Infrastructure Inspection, Agriculture, Security, Survey & Mapping, and Architecture, Engineering & Construction (AEC).

**Q: What drone services does Soko Aerial offer for mining?**
A: Equipment & operation monitoring, geotechnical & environmental monitoring, security and surveillance, incident emergency response, digital terrain modeling, and construction management. Drones replace hazardous manual inspections, increase efficiency, improve safety, and reduce costs.

**Q: How does Soko Aerial assist in disaster management?**
